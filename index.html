What is Teal4Tourette?

Teal4Tourette is an advocate-led initiative helping communities across the country light up buildings, bridges, and landmarks in teal for Tourette Syndrome Awareness Month. We offer the guide, tools, and community of experienced advocates you need to bring teal lights to your own town, and we’re looking for advocates like you to help us create a day when the whole country glows together.
